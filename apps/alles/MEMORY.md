# Remembering conversations

Memories retain a character's own evidence and uncertain reports. A timer is not a reason to repeat every
remembered utterance. Autonomous memory speech now shares witnessed events; hearsay requires a relevant
question. The ordinary conversation system still handles greetings, questions and grounded advice.
Speaking a memory records recall time but adds no salience or confidence.

Exact routine greeting forms, including the social executor's greeting and common replies, are inexpensive
reflex memories. Their salience is capped at 0.05 even after repetition, with a half-life of at most ten game
minutes. They do not enter fact retrieval or spontaneous reports. This is a conservative whole-utterance
recognizer: a greeting followed by substantive information remains ordinary speech. It is not a general
semantic classifier or a claim to recognize every language or paraphrase.

Both fallback and model formation pass through the same memory policy. Repeated creates of an identical
heard claim from the same source, subject and attribution consume their admitted perceptions but retain
one memory, without raising confidence or importance. Different sources remain separate evidence.

The attribution parser recognizes the exact `told me` and `reported that` forms used by the renderer,
including nested forms. It stores one claim, the immediate speaker and the deepest named attribution,
with the audible chain depth; reported information remains hearsay. An unknown intermediary remains
unknown. Existing retained nested claims are normalized and greeting salience is capped during decay;
changed text advances its content revision so stale interpreter proposals cannot restore it.
The full intermediate chain is not retained, and the compact display is not a verbatim transcript.

Validation includes domain tests for nested and mixed attribution, legacy normalization, greeting decay,
repeated creation, information following a greeting and conversation retrieval. The live Observatory keeper
`TestObservatory_GreetingsDoNotBecomeRecursiveNews` uses actual client SAY, repeats beyond the ingress
window and checks committed memory identity, salience and formation along with received chat.
